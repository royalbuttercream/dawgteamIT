Achievement Week 2026

Observed every November across the fraternity: a week that recognises people who have improved the quality of life for Black Americans, awards the high school essay contest, and ends with the chapter banquet.

International High School Essay Contest

Topic
How can young people use leadership, service, and innovation to create positive change in their communities?
Who may enter
College-bound high school seniors at Daegu, Osan and Humphreys high schools
Deadline
Thursday 15 October 2026, 5:00 PM
Awards
First place $500, second $300, third $200
Send to
lambdaxi1911@gmail.com

Rules

  1. Title the essay with the topic above.
  2. Original work: not previously published, not under copyright elsewhere, and not generated by an artificial intelligence tool.
  3. 700 to 750 words, typed, double-spaced, submitted by email as a Microsoft Word file.
  4. Entrants under 18 must include the parental release and consent form signed by a parent or guardian.
  5. Submit the application packet and the essay together, by the deadline, to the address above.

How to enter

  1. Register online so the committee expects your packet.
  2. Download the application packet, complete it, and collect the consent signature if you are under 18.
  3. Email the packet and your essay to lambdaxi1911@gmail.com before 5:00 PM on 15 October.

Registration form status: the chapter's Google Form currently asks visitors to sign in to a Google account and turns most people away. Until it is made public, email the address above to register and say you could not use the form.

Recognition programme and banquet

The chapter has not yet published the 2026 programme. In past years the week has included a recognition programme for community honourees, the essay contest awards, and the Achievement Week banquet. In 2024 the banquet presented the Omega Man of the Year, Citizen of the Year, Founders' Lifetime Achievement, Superior Service and Brigadier General Charles Young Military Leadership awards (RoK Hard News, November 2024).

Details will appear here when announced. In the meantime: the 2024 banquet and 2023 Achievement Week photo albums, and the write-up in the Omega Year 2025 newsletter.
