Wednesday 31 December 2025 Held

New Year's Eve Party at Dragon Hill Lodge

Lambda Xi hosts Dragon Hill Lodge's annual New Year's Eve party on Yongsan: a children's party early in the evening, the adults' party with the famous midnight balloon drop, and a breakfast buffet to start the new year. This page records the party that saw in 2026; the next one will be announced here.

Details

When
Wednesday 31 December 2025, 8:00 PM (children's party); adults' party from 10:00 PM
Where
Dragon Hill Lodge, Yongsan (map)
What
DJs, multiple dance floors, bars, finger food, midnight balloon drop
Ages
Children's party for ages 5 to 12 with an accompanying adult. Adults' party: 18 or older to attend; 21 or older to drink.
Tickets
Special pricing through the chapter. The poster says "contact your Omega group representative"; use the contact page and choose "Events" if you do not have one.

Next year

The party runs every New Year's Eve. When the chapter publishes the 2026 edition it will appear on the events page and here. Photographs from past parties are in the gallery (see the 2023 "DHL New Year's Eve Party" album).
