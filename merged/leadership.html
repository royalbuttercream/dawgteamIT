Leadership and Lineage

A complete record of Lambda Xi history in one place: our current executive officers, the Basilei who have guided the chapter since 1977, and every line crossed from Fall 1977 through Fall 2025

Inaugural Executive Council

Chartered on February 22, 1977, at the Eighth United States Army Headquarters in Yongsan, the chapter established its foundation under its inaugural Executive Council:

  • Basileus: Brother Alphonso Maldon
  • Vice Basileus: Brother Joe Howze
  • Keeper of Records and Seal: Brother Willie Hensley
  • Keeper of Finance: Brother William Blakely
  • Keeper of Peace: Brother Charles Burton
  • Chaplain: Brother Herschel Jones
  • Chapter Editor: Brother Freddie Price
