Lambda Xi at 50: Golden Jubilee Celebration

Chartered on February 22, 1977, in Yongsan, Lambda Xi Chapter stands as the first Black Greek Letter Organization on the Korean Peninsula. Join us for a historic anniversary weekend honoring 50 years of legacy, service, and brotherhood.
