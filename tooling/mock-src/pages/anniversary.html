Fifty years in brief

Lambda Xi was the fifth Omega Psi Phi chapter chartered outside the continental United States. Eleven charter members and an inaugural council led by Brother Alphonso Maldon founded it at the Eighth United States Army Headquarters. Thirty-one Basilei have led the chapter since; it has been 13th District Graduate Chapter of the Year in 2015 and every year from 2023 to 2026.
