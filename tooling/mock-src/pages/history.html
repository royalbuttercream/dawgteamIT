Roots at Howard, 1911

Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. was founded at Howard University on 17 November 1911. The national history is told on the fraternity's own site: About Omega, oppf.org. intro text to approve

The charter, 1977

The year 1977 proved to be an exciting one for the international expansion of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, as two chapters, on opposite sides of the world, were chartered six months apart. As only the fifth chapter to be chartered outside of the continental United States, Lambda Xi would call the Republic of Korea (South Korea) its home. This brotherly expansion under Grand Basileus Edward J. Braynon was a celebration for the Fraternity and the Republic of Korea, as the brotherhood had begun to make its presence known years earlier.

The original application was initiated through the Third District Representative, Brother B.T. Garnett, on 20 October 1974; on 23 May 1975, Brother Harold J. Cook, National Executive Secretary, notified Brother LTC. Leroy C. Bell of the charter application approval. Less than two years later, on 22 February 1977, Lambda Xi was chartered at the Eighth United States Army Headquarters, United States Army Garrison Yongsan, Republic of Korea.

The Lambda Xi Chapter of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Incorporated, was the first Black Greek Letter Organization to be activated on the Korean Peninsula. For nearly five decades, the Men of Lambda Xi have proudly served with distinction on the Korean Peninsula. We fully embrace our responsibilities in the Korean/US community. Whether assisting the less fortunate or uplifting those striving for academic excellence, the wide range of talents and expertise amongst the brotherhood enables us to routinely take on tasks that most deem too difficult to accomplish.

Article text

The ever widening hand of Omega has reached across the Pacific Ocean to establish its first Chapter in Korea on the Northeast Asia Peninsula. As the result of hardwork by dedicated Omega men who have served with the United States Armed forces in Korea, Lambda Xi Chapter has become a reality.

News of the chartering of the chapter by the Supreme Council on February 22, 1977, resulted in a colorful ceremony at the Seoul American Embassy Club at which the charter was presented and the first officers installed. Guests included Omega wives, three potential pledgees and military friends.

The first order of the day by the first Basileus, Capt. Alphonso Maldon, II, was to extend warm heartfelt greetings to the Supreme Council and all chapters in the fraternity from the brothers who proudly established Omega in the "Land of the Morning Calm."

Other officers are: Lt. Joe D. Howze, Vice-Basileus; GS-11 James H. Campbell, KF; Capt. William L. Hensley, KRS; Capt. Herschel L. Jones, Chaplain; Capt. Freddie Price, Editor to the ORACLE; Capt. William Blakely, Dean of Pledgees; and Maj. Charles H. Burton, Jr., Keeper of Peace. Other members are Capt. Curtis A. Baylor, 2Lt. Edward L. Denson, and 1st Lt. Tommy Hunter.

The chapter is currently involved in improving the relationship between Black Americans and Koreans of the Military Community. Projects include a permanent fraternity house and financial and physical support to the Orphans (Black-Korean) programs.
