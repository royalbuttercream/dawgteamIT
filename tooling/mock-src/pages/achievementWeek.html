Achievement Week 2026

Observed fraternity-wide every November, Achievement Week recognizes individuals who advance the quality of life for Black Americans, awards high school essay contest winners, and concludes with our annual chapter banquet.

International High School Essay Contest

Topic prompt

How can young people use leadership, service, and innovation to create positive change in their communities?

Eligibility
College-bound high school seniors at Daegu, Osan, and Humphreys high schools
Deadline
{{event:essayDeadline2026:when}}
Awards
1st place $500 · 2nd place $300 · 3rd place $200

Rules and formatting

  • Must be 700 to 750 words, typed, double-spaced, and submitted as a Microsoft Word file (.docx).
  • Essay title must match the prompt topic exactly.
  • Must be original work (no artificial intelligence tools, prior publications, or copyrighted text).
  • Entrants under 18 must include the signed parental release and consent form.

How to enter

  1. Register online: complete the registration form below so the committee expects your application.
  2. Prepare your packet: download and complete the application packet (including parental consent if under 18).
  3. Submit: email your completed packet and essay together to the Scholarship Committee before 5:00 PM on October 15.
