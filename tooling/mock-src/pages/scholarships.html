Programs

Scholarships

Two scholarships for high school seniors, awarded every year. Applications are accepted from 1 February to 1 May.

  • $2,500 · three students

    Future Leaders Scholarship

    Open to high school seniors who intend to pursue a Bachelor's Degree at any accredited college or university.

    Application form (PDF, 1 page, 150 KB)

  • $3,000 · one student

    Bro. Carl Reed Scholarship

    Open to high school seniors who intend to pursue a Bachelor's Degree at a historically Black college or university (HBCU).

    Application form (PDF, 1 page, 155 KB)

How to apply

  1. Download the application form for the scholarship you are applying for and complete it. Both forms ask for the same details; you may apply for both.
  2. Gather your supporting documents: transcript and the items the form lists.
  3. Submit between 1 February and 1 May, either online or by email.

Submit online

What the online form will ask

The fields below match the chapter's current online application so nothing changes for applicants when the Google Form replaces it.

  • Which scholarship (Future Leaders, Bro. Carl Reed, or both)
  • Student's first and last name
  • School (Daegu, Osan, Humphreys, or other)
  • Student email
  • Parent or guardian name, phone (Korea +82 or US +1) and email
  • Upload of the completed application form and transcript (PDF, up to 10 MB)

Submit by email

Send the completed form and your documents as PDF attachments to scholarship@lambdaxi1911.com with the subject "Scholarship application, [your name]". You will receive an acknowledgement; the committee announces awards after the 1 May close.

What happens next

The Scholarship Committee reviews every complete application received by 1 May. Recipients are notified by email and recognised at a chapter event. Questions: the same address.
