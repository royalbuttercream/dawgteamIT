2025 to 2026 Omega Year

Executive Officers

Eight brothers hold the chapter's offices for the Omega Year that began in November 2025. Each card names the office and the brother and, behind a disclosure, says what the office does.

  • Brother Eugene Gibbs

    Basileus

    About the office

    Presides over the chapter, chairs meetings and represents Lambda Xi to the 13th District and the fraternity. Read his message.

  • Brother Richard Smith

    Immediate Past Basileus

    About the office

    Basileus for the 2024 to 2025 year; advises the current council and keeps continuity between terms.

  • Brother Randy Artis

    Vice Basileus

    About the office

    Deputises for the Basileus and coordinates the chapter's programmes and committees.

  • Brother Charlesvester Wims

    Keeper of Records and Seal

    About the office

    The chapter's secretary: minutes, membership records, correspondence and the chapter seal.

  • Brother Marcus Shepard

    Keeper of Finance

    About the office

    The chapter's treasurer: dues, budgets, scholarship and programme funds, and financial reports.

  • Brother Andrew Wesley

    Chaplain

    About the office

    Leads devotions at meetings and events, and the Memorial Service each 12 March for brothers who have entered Omega Chapter.

  • Brother Clint Carmichael

    Keeper of Peace

    About the office

    Keeps order at meetings and events, the fraternity's equivalent of a sergeant-at-arms.

  • Brother Michael Robinson

    Editor to the Oracle

    About the office

    The chapter's correspondent to The Oracle, the fraternity's magazine, and editor of the chapter newsletter, RoK Hard News.
