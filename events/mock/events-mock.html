Every year

Dates change each year; these are the fixtures visitors can plan around.

1 February to 1 May
Scholarship applications open
12 March
Memorial Service for brothers who have entered Omega Chapter
September
All Star basketball tournament
October
Youth Leadership Conference; essay contest deadline
November
Achievement Week: recognition programme, essay awards and banquet
31 December
New Year's Eve Party at Dragon Hill Lodge

Dates, venues and eligibility come from the event posters on lambdaxi1911.com as captured on 23 September 2026. The yearly fixtures are inferred from those posters and the mandated programme calendar; confirm the March and September dates with the chapter.
