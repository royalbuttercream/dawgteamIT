The first council, 1977

When the chapter was chartered on 22 February 1977 at the Eighth United States Army Headquarters, Yongsan, its inaugural Executive Council was:

  • Brother Alphonso Maldon, Basileus
  • Brother Joe Howze, Vice Basileus
  • Brother Willie Hensley, Keeper of Records and Seal
  • Brother William Blakely, Keeper of Finance
  • Brother Charles Burton, Keeper of Peace
  • Brother Herschel Jones, Chaplain
  • Brother Freddie Price, Chapter Editor

The eleven charter members and the story of the charter are on the History page.
