Saturday 17 October 2026

2026 Youth Leadership Conference

Lead today. Impact tomorrow. Inspire forever. A free one-day conference for young men and women in grades 9 to 12, presented by the Lambda Xi Chapter of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. and the RoK Alumnae Chapter of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.

Details

When
Saturday 17 October 2026, 11:00 AM
Where
Humphreys Middle School, Camp Humphreys (map)
Who
Young men and women in grades 9 to 12
Cost
Free. Places are limited to the first 60 students who register.
Sessions
Leadership development; teamwork and collaboration; community service; goal setting and success; financial literacy

About the conference

The conference is the chapter's annual youth programme on the Peninsula, run with the RoK Alumnae Chapter of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc. It sits alongside the fraternity's mandated Fatherhood and Mentoring, Scholarship and Social Action programmes. See Mandated Programs for the wider picture and the gallery for photographs from previous years.
